# Serene HRIS & School Management - Full System Changelog

## System Architecture & Massive Module Rollout (June 2026)
This application has undergone a massive architectural scale-up to support a complete, end-to-end ERP and School Management Ecosystem. We have migrated from a simple dashboard to a 30+ module monolith with extensive UI/UX polishing.

### Feature Completeness by Domain:

**1. Core Academic & EdTech System**
- **PPDB (Admissions)**: Full candidate tracking workflow and pipeline evaluation.
- **Digital LMS**: Rebuilt the "Digital LMS & Learning" module to support full-scale hybrid learning. Implemented an expansive overview Dashboard tracking learner engagement and assessment scores via interactive Recharts Area/Bar charts. Engineered the "Course Manager" view with course cards, enrollment stats, and syllabus management. Integrated a deeply structured "Enterprise Video Library" simulating Adaptive Bitrate Streaming (ABS) for hosting thousands of academic lectures and boarding halaqahs, alongside categorised thumbnail indexing. Delivered an "Assessments" registry seamlessly integrated with the core CBT system.
- **CBT System**: Live exam supervision (CBT Manager) and secure testing interface (CBT Student Dashboard).
- **Tahfidz**: Specialized Quranic memorization tracker & assessment.
- **Library**: Completely modernized the "Library Manager" module replacing basic catalogs with advanced metrics. Implemented a robust "Dashboard" combining statistical KPIs with AreaCharts tracking multi-channel access trends (Physical vs Digital) and PieCharts breaking down popular genres. Upgraded the "Data Buku" registry into an expanded table with availability tracking, precise shelf location, and direct checkout indicators. Programmed an intensive "Smart Circulation" console featuring QR code staging for rapid asset scanning and a real-time overdue fines monitoring system. Debuted the expansive "Digital Lib" portal simulating access telemetry across e-books and multimedia artifacts wrapped within an ultra-modern heroic gradient UI.

**2. Human Resources (HRIS)**
- **Attendance Kiosk & Geofencing**: Live kiosk view with `navigator.geolocation` tracking.
- **Check-In Locations Map**: `react-leaflet` map implementation visualizing staff check-ins dynamically, featuring interactive clickable markers revealing an enhanced contextual side-panel with robust address strings and checking methods.
- **Leave Request & Time Off**: Request workflows and policy tracking.
- **Performance Scorecard**: Visual Radar Charts evaluating compound KPIs for every employee.
- **Shift Planner**: Automated & manual tabular shift structuring.

**3. Administration & AI-Powered Document Management**
- **AI Document OCR**: Implemented `/api/ocr` backend endpoint using Gemini-3.5-pro to intelligently extract metadata from student credentials and invoices via drag-and-drop.
- **E-Office & Communication**: Unified communication hubs and document repositories.

**4. Operations & Boarding**
- **Asset Manager**: Asset lifecycle registry, distribution pie charts & expenditure bar charts (Recharts), and a QR-labeled details panel for maintaining equipment schedules. Integrated real-time QR Code scanning via device camera for rapid asset lookup alongside dynamic code generation for each catalog registry entry. Added a print-optimized labels generation layout compatible with `window.print()` that formats the generated IDs in a physical sticker matrix. Created a unified `LogMaintenanceModal` to interactively link, track, and statefully add repairs or inspections. Added interactive Check-in/Check-out assignment tracking built directly into the QR scanner's detailed asset modal to seamlessly manage custody transfers. Added "Add Asset" modal to allow creating and listing new assets in real-time.
- **Boarding Manager**: Full tracking for dorm administration and discipline.
- **Healthcare Manager**: Clinical logs for student/staff ailments.
- **Laundry & Cooperative (Koperasi)**: POS interfaces, inventory, and trackable laundry limits.
- **Procurement Manager**: Deployed complete procurement lifecycle module featuring a unified dashboard with spend analytics (Recharts line & pie charts). Included integrated tabs for drafting and tracking "Purchase Requests" (PRs) across departments, managing "Purchase Orders" (POs) lifecycles with one-click "Mark Fulfilled" capabilities, and maintaining a robust Vendor Directory for evaluation and sourcing reliability. Introduced deeply-integrated "Vendor Profile" modals to track individual contact information, calculate structural performance ratings (via stars), and automatically aggregate contract history referencing fulfilled PO databases. Added interactive "Create PO" workflows inside the Purchase Orders tab, supporting selection from active suppliers and providing stateful transitions from "Draft" to "Issued" to "Fulfilled", complete with a visual step-indicator modal tracking system for easy review. Implemented Purchase Order Auto-Generation, allowing managers to review pending PRs, instantly assign an active vendor, and automatically convert the approved PR into a drafted PO linked to the vendor's profile in a single click.
- **E-Document Manager**: Advanced E-Office implementation across 5 sub-modules (Surat Masuk, Surat Keluar, E-Approval, Arsip Digital, and AI OCR). Activated interactive forms to "Register Incoming" documents (logging sender workflows alongside "Disposed" tracking), and to "Draft Outgoing" correspondence (tracking state dynamically from 'Draft' to 'Waiting Approval'). Developed the deep "E-Approval" queue pipeline featuring interactive document preview modals with one-click "Sign & Approve" capabilities utilizing dummy QR code signature tagging. Centralized historical policy and MoU contracts tracking in a structured "Archive" view.

**5. Finance & Extended Enterprise**
- **Finance Manager**: Cash flows, general ledger, and integrated tuition billing arrays.
- **Donation Management**: Tracking incoming endowments and campaigns.
- **Enterprise, Ecosystem & ERP**: Multi-branch administration and corporate overview architecture.
- **Executive Intelligence Center**: Developed a comprehensive "Executive Intelligence Center" incorporating high-level AI-driven analytics. Redesigned the "Executive Dashboard" to present anomaly detection alarms and KPI summary indicators. Introduced a state-of-the-art "AI Tahfidz Engine" simulating voice-biometric live scoring (Tajwid, Makhraj, Fluency) combined with algorithmic spaced-repetition logic. Constructed the "Predictive Analytics" wing utilizing Recharts to forecast student enrollment pipelines and tuition default risks. Fully activated the "Data Warehouse" layout exhibiting an interactive SQL runner and ETL pipeline health statuses. Delivered a powerful "Knowledge Hub" interface deploying Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) paradigms for semantic SOP and syllabus search indexing.
- **Ecosystem & PPOB**: Developed the "Ecosystem Services & PPOB" module integrating multi-service utility transactions, ZISWAF fund distribution analysis, and future-savings trackers. Added a comprehensive KPI Dashboard containing Recharts AreaCharts to monitor monthly PPOB volume alongside a detailed PieChart distributing ZISWAF receipts. Created the "PPOB Manager" pane summarizing 6 distinct biller gateways and listing recent robust transaction logs. Packaged a "ZISWAF Gateway" displaying campaign cards with graphical progress bars towards their respective targets. Executed a specialized "Umroh & Qurban Savings" tracker that structures participant progress against targeted financial goals.
- **Marketplace**: Developed the "DPesantren Marketplace" connecting parents, alumni, and the public to student entrepreneurship products and uniform sales. Implemented a robust "Storefront" mimicking e-commerce navigation with item categorization, review ratings, and dynamic search formatting. Built an "Orders" managerial tracking tab using visual status indicators (Clock, Truck, CheckCircle) to systematically progress orders from 'Pending' through 'Delivered'. Produced a comprehensive "Analytics" panel plotting weekly revenue velocity arrays via dynamic Recharts AreaCharts and top-level KPI metrics cards (Revenue, Avg. Order Value, Conversion Rate).
- **CRM & Alumni**: Promoted the "Alumni Directory & Network" module to feature advanced analytics and networking views. Integrated a full-featured "Tracer Study" view utilizing Recharts for precise graduation trends and activity pipelines. Added an interactive "Community & Events" hub containing dynamic job-boards and reunion scheduling records. Activated deep "Donation & Endowment" dashboards linking historical contributions to large-scale funding targets with progress tracking visuals. Implemented the complete "Parent CRM & Donor Relations" lifecycle management module. Included a contact directory with cross-category (Parent/Prospect/Donor/Alumni) segmentation. Added visual pipeline boards tracking lead acquisition through enrollment and retention. Delivered an integrated Unified Broadcast Engine capable of synthesizing email and messaging campaign logic with granular metrics reporting. Provided interaction demographic visualizers utilizing rich bar/pie-charts.
